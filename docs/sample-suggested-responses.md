# Market Pulse — Sample Suggested Responses

These are reference examples of what `lib/claude-analysis.js` `draftResponses()`
produces. All are drafts — a human approves/edits before anything is posted.
They follow the §8 rules: local and human, no competitor attacks, no false
urgency, no unverified claims, no sight-unseen diagnosis, contact info only where
it fits.

### fb_group_public · spring_repair · same_day
> Sorry about the spring — that's one of the more common ones we see, and usually a
> same-day fix. We're local here in DeSoto County. Happy to take a look and give
> you a straight quote before any work. Feel free to message me.

### private_message · company_recommendation · local_company
> Hey! Saw your post looking for a garage door company. We're a small local crew —
> two trucks, based right here. No pressure and no upsell games: we'll inspect,
> tell you what's actually going on, and give you a flat quote. If it helps I can
> swing by this week to take a look.

### urgent_repair · emergency · safety_concern
> If the door is stuck partway or a cable/spring let go, it's worth not forcing it —
> those can be under a lot of tension. We can get someone out to assess it safely.
> Give us a call at {ITG_PHONE} and we'll sort out timing.

### nextdoor · opener_repair · professional_diagnosis
> Openers that just click can be a few different things — sometimes the opener,
> sometimes the door binding and tripping the safety. Worth having someone confirm
> before replacing the whole unit. We're local and can take a look if you'd like.

### google_review · referral_thanks
> Thank you for the kind words — it means a lot to a small local team. We're glad
> the door's working smoothly again, and we're here if you ever need us.

### price_sensitive · financing
> Totally understand wanting to keep it reasonable. We give a flat quote up front
> so there are no surprises, and we'll walk you through what's actually necessary
> vs. what can wait. Happy to take a look and lay out the options.

### after_hours · same_day
> Thanks for reaching out — we've got your message and will follow up first thing.
> If it's an emergency (door won't close, security concern), call {ITG_PHONE} and
> we'll see what we can do tonight.

### safety_focused · no_pressure_inspection
> A door that's off-track or making a grinding noise is worth checking sooner than
> later so it doesn't turn into a bigger repair. No pressure — we can do a quick
> inspection and tell you honestly whether it needs work now or can wait.

---

**What these deliberately avoid:** naming or knocking competitors; "act now"
pressure; promising a same-day slot before it's confirmed; diagnosing the exact
fault without seeing the door; dropping the phone number into every reply. Public
comments stay short; private messages run a little longer.
